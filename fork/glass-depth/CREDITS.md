# glass-depth: wallpaper credits

Every picture here comes from [couch-tv](https://github.com/Seagey/couch-tv) by Seagey (MIT), whose Night home
screen this theme recreates. The pictures were cropped from couch-tv's 4K copies and resized to 1920x1080; each
`-fg.webp` is a cut-out of the scenery (the sky transparent) drawn over the clock, and each `-glass.jpg` is a
blurred, brightened copy the clock's digits are filled with.
